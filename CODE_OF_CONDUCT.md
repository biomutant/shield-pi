# Code of Conduct

Shield Pi welcomes technical discussion and contributions from people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and behavior.
- Assume good faith while still asking for reproducible evidence.
- Give contributors room to disagree on architecture and implementation.
- Keep reviews specific and actionable.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, private logs, or private images.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately humiliate other participants.

## Project discussions

Technical disagreement is normal. The preferred way to resolve it is with code, measurements, logs, tests, and clearly stated trade-offs.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, spammy, off-topic, or privacy-violating content and may restrict participation when needed to keep the project usable and safe.

For conduct concerns that should not be discussed publicly, use an available private GitHub reporting/contact mechanism rather than posting personal details in an issue.
